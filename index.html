How it decides

  
    
    
  1. Look at the pixels
    The photo is re-saved as a JPEG and compared with the original. Pasted or retouched areas recompress unevenly, which shows up as an edit-trace map.
    2. 
    
  2. Check the details
    Aadhaar numbers are tested with the Verhoeff checksum, PAN and passport numbers against their format rules, and dates of birth and names for plausibility.
    3. 
    
  3. Score it together
    A logistic-regression model weighs all six signals and returns one probability, plus how much each signal contributed.
    4. 
  






  
Privacy and limits

  
Images are analysed on your device and never sent anywhere. This demo model is trained on synthetic examples when the page loads, so use it to explore the approach, not to make real onboarding decisions. A production system needs a labelled dataset of real genuine and forged documents, face and liveness checks, and verification against the issuing authority.
